# Project name

Copy this to an ignored file under `projects/` when an active project needs durable context. Replace prompts with supplied facts and sources; omit empty sections.

For an unlinked project, use Current state through Sources and open questions below. Once a workspace is configured, use Linked workspace through Strategic sources and questions instead. Copy only the relevant variant; follow the [Linked project workspaces rules](../AGENTS.md#linked-project-workspaces).

## Current state

State the problem, what has actually changed, and the observation date. Keep a proposal, approval, deployment, adoption, and measured impact distinct.

## Commitment and ownership

Name the agreed owner, expected outcome, delivery date, and dependencies when known. Mark proposed ownership or an unknown deadline honestly.

## Operating loop

Locate the work: problem validated, commitment legible, delivery steered, impact measured, or outcome communicated. Name the next gate. For shipped work, record agreed adoption checks, impact measurement, and communication dates; leave unresolved dates explicit.

## Sources and open questions

Link the primary evidence and relevant local source archive. Record what would change the current read. Do not turn every meeting note into another entry.

## Linked workspace

Link the workspace's configured status file. Keep the workspace mapping in `sources.local.md`; read delivery state, owners, dates, and post-launch follow-up from that source.

## Strategic read

Explain why the project matters, its operating-loop stage and next gate, stakeholder or political considerations, and cross-project dependencies. Record material calls with their date, confidence, and what would falsify them. Keep delivery detail in the workspace.

## Strategic sources and questions

Link evidence supporting the strategic read and the relevant local source archive. Record the strategic unknowns that could change the assessment.
