# Person name

Copy this to an ignored file under `one-on-ones/`, named for the person in lowercase with hyphens, such as `one-on-ones/alex-rivera.md`. Replace the prompts with real content. The `one_on_one.py` script reads the headings and line shapes below, so keep them.

**Cadence:** weekly

Use `weekly`, `fortnightly`, `monthly`, or `every N days`. Leave it out if no rhythm is agreed.

## Current picture

Two to five lines on what is lasting: what works for this person, what keeps recurring, what they care about. Rewrite this section when the picture changes. Do not append to it.

## Open asks

One line per ask they have made of you. Open asks begin with an empty box and the date they were raised. Say what you agreed in brackets. Close an ask by marking the box and adding the date.

- [ ] YYYY-MM-DD What they asked for (you said: your response, or "not yet answered")
- [x] YYYY-MM-DD A closed ask (done YYYY-MM-DD)

## Log

Newest first. One entry per meeting. The source is `gemini`, `notes`, or `memory`.

### YYYY-MM-DD · source

**What's working:** Their answer, in their words where you have them. Write "Not discussed" if it did not come up.

**What's frustrating:** Their answer. Observed statements only. Put your interpretation in a separate line starting "My read:".

**What they need from me:** Their answer. Each concrete ask also becomes a line under Open asks.

**Other:** Anything else material, such as a decision or a follow-up you raised. Omit if empty.
